# AGENTS.md

Guidance for AI coding agents working in this repo. Keep it terse.

## Read first

- **`CONTEXT.md`**: domain glossary (pt-BR terms plus their code names). Use its terms; its `_Avoid_` lists are binding.
- **`docs/adr/`**: decisions that look surprising without context. Do not "fix" them silently.

## Structure

Turborepo + pnpm (hoisted `node_modules`), mirroring `www/monorepo`.

- `apps/server`: Express 5 + Apollo Server (`/graphql`) + Better Auth (`/api/auth`) + Bot chat (`POST /api/chat/:conversationId`, server-sent events). Bots run in `src/services/bots`: the catalog (`catalog.ts`), what each Bot knows (`context.ts`), its prompt, the chat loop and the Job runner (`jobs.ts`, in-process, ADR 0010). They reach Models only through `src/services/models.ts`, which serves them from OpenRouter.
- `packages/bots/*`: one package per Bot, with its catalog entry and its pure logic. No I/O there; database and Model calls stay in `apps/server`.
  - `packages/bots/finance` (`@zapbots/finance`): Caishen, the finance Bot. Pure financial math (amortization, Implied Rate, Assessment) with tests; all financial logic lives here.
- `packages/database`: Drizzle schema, migrations (`drizzle/`), lazy `db` client.
- `packages/auth`: Better Auth instance + `resolveViewer(headers)`.
- `packages/graphql`: SDL schema, client operations (`src/operations/*.graphql`), codegen output (`src/generated`, gitignored).
- `packages/client`: React + Vite + Apollo Client 4. Vite proxies `/api` and `/graphql` so every request shares one origin and one session cookie.
  - UI is shadcn/ui on Tailwind v4. Every control or surface (button, input, select, table, card, alert…) is a shadcn component from `src/components/ui`. Add missing ones with `pnpm dlx shadcn@latest add <name>` from `packages/client`, then point their `cn` import at `@/lib/utils`. Never use raw `<button>`/`<input>`/`<select>`/`<table>`.
  - Layout (`Layout`): a navy frame, one piece: the header across the top (`AppHeader`: the mark, then on the right the button that opens the chat (⌘K/Ctrl+K opens it too, focused; hidden on `/chat`) and Alerts) and the menu down the left (`AppSidebar`, from `useNav` in `lib/nav.ts`: Página inicial and Agente; then Assistentes: every working Bot (leading to its page) with its pages (`BOT_SECTIONS`) always listed under it, then `UPCOMING_BOTS` greyed out with "Em breve"; at the foot, the avatar (`ProfileMenu`, shadcn's NavUser: `Avatar` with the Google photo or the initials) with Perfil, Plano, Organização, Integrações, Idioma and Sair). The Dashboard is only the greeting and the line to ask the Caishen AI. The screen is a rounded light panel inside the frame, with the chat panel (`ChatPanel`, a `BotPanel`, animated with `motion`) docked on its right. Every path is in English: Dashboard `/`, Agente `/chat` (a page only for the chat, with its own column of Conversations and nothing else; the panel stays closed there), Workflows (`/workflows`, the UI name of Jobs), Documentos (`/documents`) and Score de crédito (`/caishen/credit`) are off: out of the menu, their addresses redirect, their pages (`JobsPage`, `DocumentsPage`, `DocumentPage`, `CreditPage`) kept for later; `/caishen/*`, `/integrations`, `/organization`, `/plan`, `/profile`, `/sign-in`, and the landing page at `/` signed out and `/landing` always.
  - Each working Bot has a page (`BotLayout`, e.g. `/caishen`) with tabs from `BOT_SECTIONS` in `lib/bots.ts` (Visão geral, and Financeiro `/caishen/finances` with Salário, Impostos and Obrigações as parts of one page, `#income`, `#taxes`, `#obligations`); the chat is not a tab; Conversations start from the chat panel (the header button or ⌘K/Ctrl+K) or the Agente page. `data-bot` picks the Bot's ink (`text-ink`, `bg-ink`, `border-ink`). `TEAM` lists only Bots that work. Bots still being built (Caishen Personal, Caishen Work) are `UPCOMING_BOTS`: shown disabled with "Em breve" in the menu and on the landing page, with no `BotKey`, pages or server side until they work.
  - The Caishen's pages (`BOT_SECTIONS`): Lançamentos (`/caishen/entries`, where `/caishen` opens; `/caishen/overview` and `/caishen/report` redirect) is the month as a sheet with, under it, `ReportCharts` (six months from `Holder.months`, Gastos by category, debt per Operation); its tab Fixos (`/caishen/entries/fixed`) lists what goes out every month: Gastos recorrentes, Empréstimos e cartões (`#obligations`; Operations open on `/caishen/operations/:id`) and Impostos. Lançamentos has four tabs (`EntriesTabs`): Mês, Entradas (`/caishen/entries/income`, `IncomePage`: Fontes de Renda and the month's payments, month by month), Saídas fixas (`/caishen/entries/fixed`) and Impostos (`/caishen/entries/taxes`, `TaxesPage`, ADR 0016: for whoever registers a CNPJ, the Empresa with its regime (MEI, Simples, Presumido; Real not worked out), the pró-labore as a payslip, each competência's Faturamento and RBT12 and the guides it owes; math in `@zapbots/finance` `company.ts`, guides in `services/company.ts`, shown in the month they fall due as `COMPANY_TAX` lines). A Fonte de Renda can be in dollars (ADR 0015): `services/income.ts` splits the month over the paydays and converts each at the PTAX de compra (`services/exchange.ts`, kept in `exchange_rate`) or the User's rate (`income_rate_override`), less spread and IOF (`foreignToBrlCents`). Month lines are not normalized by Apollo (`MonthLine: { keyFields: false }`): one Fonte de Renda can be two lines with one id. Everything is added through one dialog, `AddEntryDialog` (`AddEntryButton`: "Adicionar entrada" and "Adicionar saída" at the top of Lançamentos; one of them on Entradas and Saídas fixas), opened on its side, then the kind (Receita avulsa, Renda fixa; Gasto, Gasto recorrente, Empréstimo, Financiamento, Cartão, Imposto) and its form. In the sheet, typed or imported lines (Gastos, Receitas avulsas) are edited in their cells (`SheetCell`, saved through `updateRecord`) and deleted alone or in a selection; lines from elsewhere link to it and delete their source after a warning (`ConfirmDelete`).
  - Lançamentos (`/caishen/entries`, `EntriesPage`): one sheet over a period picked at the top: a month walked with arrows (`?m=YYYY-MM`, `Holder.month`), the last 15 or 30 days, the last 3, 6 or 12 months (whole, the current one included) or two dates (`?p=custom&from=&to=`); any period goes through `Holder.period(from, to)` (`periodStatement`: the months it touches, cut to its days, up to two years). Each month holds Receitas (Fontes de Renda in force and Receitas avulsas, `oneOffIncome`), Compromissos due (Installments, Statements, Impostos via `taxDueInMonth`, Gastos recorrentes via `recurringDueInMonth`; those on a Cartão go to `onCard`, shown and not summed, as their Fatura counts them), Gastos (`expense`, typed in or imported from .csv/.xlsx by `lib/spreadsheet.ts`; in a statement with both signs only the negative lines are Gastos) and the balance; it exports as CSV, or prints to PDF (`print:` classes hide the menu, header and chat).
  - Integrações is one page (`/integrations`, `ConnectionsSettings`, opened from the avatar menu): a search and a Situação filter, then a list per kind in `INTEGRATION_GROUPS` (`lib/integrations.ts`, five kinds: `#ai` Modelos de IA, OpenRouter and the Model of each Bot; `#messages` Comunicação; `#files` Arquivos, Google Drive; `#finance` Bancos e finanças; `#productivity` Produtividade e saúde), what works in full and what is "em breve" in one line each. Only Integrations that work are listed. Financeiro and Integrações share `Sheet`/`SheetRow` (`components/Sheet.tsx`). There are no Configurações: Organização (name and Users), Plano (billing and usage) and Perfil (name, language) are pages of their own.
  - UI strings go through `useI18n().t(key)` (`lib/i18n.tsx`, pt and en; signed in, the language is the User's `language` field, set on Perfil; before that, per browser, Portuguese by default). Screens not moved there yet are pt-BR only; new strings go in the dictionary.
  - The look (`src/styles.css`), after Hostinger's hPanel: navy `#0F172A`/`#1E293B` for the header and the menu, a slate-white screen `#F8FAFC` with white cards and `#E2E8F0` borders, one bright blue `#2563EB` for what acts (`--primary`, and the Caishen's ink), Inter everywhere, tabular figures, and `.page-enter` (a short fade sliding down) on each page container keyed by path. Components use the tokens, never hex. There is no dark theme.
  - Sign-in has no password: a link sent by email (`authClient.signIn.magicLink`, Better Auth's `magicLink` plugin; sent through Resend when `RESEND_API_KEY` is set; otherwise printed on the server console and, when `CLIENT_ORIGIN` is localhost, handed back to the page by `POST /api/auth/sign-in/local-link`) and Google (`authClient.signIn.social`), offered only when `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`/`GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET` are set. `GET /api/auth/sign-in-methods` tells the page which are on. The Google Drive Connection asks for the `drive.file` scope later, on the Integrações page, and its file picker needs `VITE_GOOGLE_API_KEY`/`VITE_GOOGLE_APP_ID`.
  - Landing page (`LandingPage`, pt-BR only): what exists today (Caishen Finance, .csv/.xlsx import, a Price-table simulator on `priceInstallment`, a Visão geral mockup with example figures); Personal, Work, OFX and Open Finance only under "Em breve". Mockups are HTML, not screenshots.
  - Error monitoring: Sentry (`apps/server/src/sentry.ts`, imported first by `app.ts`; `packages/client/src/lib/sentry.ts`, imported first by `main.tsx`), on only when `SENTRY_DSN` / `VITE_SENTRY_DSN` are set. Errors only, and no User data: cookies, bodies, GraphQL variables, Model prompts and local variables stay out (`dataCollection`). Report errors we catch and answer ourselves with `reportError`.
  - Billing (ADR 0011): Plans and Limits in `services/billing/plans.ts`, Stripe over REST in `services/billing/stripe.ts` (Checkout, portal, webhook at `POST /api/billing/webhook`, raw body, signature checked, events deduped). Needs `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, `STRIPE_PRICE_PRO`, `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`. Jobs, Routines and chat Messages pass `requireWithinLimit`. Usage codes are created with `pnpm usage-code`.

Workspace packages have no build step: `main` points at `src/index.ts`; apps run with `tsx`. Production is one Vercel project with two Services, `client` and `server` (`vercel.json`, ADR 0012): `apps/server/src/app.ts` is the Express app, `index.ts` the long-lived process around it. The server's build runs `pnpm db:migrate` before codegen, so each deploy brings the production database up to the code (migrations must stay additive: a new column nullable or with a default).

## Conventions

- Code in English, UI in pt-BR.
- Money is integer cents (`number`, `bigint` in Postgres); rates are monthly effective fractions. Money math uses `decimal.js` in `@zapbots/finance`, never `float` arithmetic (ADR 0004).
- Every query or mutation scopes by `viewer.organizationId`: build the filter with `owned(table, viewer, id?)` from `resolvers/guards.ts`, never by hand, and load a Holder through `findHolder`. The Organization is personal and has its own page; changing it takes `requireAdmin`. Finanças uses the `viewerHolder` (ADR 0007).
- Bots only read (ADR 0009): nothing written outside zapbots, no money moved. They reach outside tools only through Connections (OAuth), never with stored passwords. Give Models money already formatted.
- In the chat, a Model's only tools propose records: new ones, corrections or deletions of saved ones (ADR 0013, `services/bots/proposals.ts`): a tool call stores a PENDING Proposal; only `confirmProposal` writes, through the services in `services/records.ts` (`updateRecord` for corrections, `deleteRecord` for deletions). Values arrive as people type them and are read by `inputFor` (`services/recordForms.ts`), the same for the Model, the Proposal card (`editProposal`) and the Editar dialog of saved records (`recordFields`/`updateRecord`, `EditRecordDialog`); the Holder comes from the session. New write paths go in `records.ts`, shared by resolver and Proposal. A deletion, too, happens only on the User's confirmation.
- A Bot is an entry in `CATALOG` (`services/bots/catalog.ts`) with its name and personality fixed in its package; the `bot` table only stores which Organization has it and its Model (ADR 0010).
- A Job runs in the server process; `createJob` starts it and the client polls `job`. On boot, unfinished Jobs are marked failed; on Vercel, `failStaleJobs` does it to Jobs past their function's time limit (ADR 0012). Hand Jobs over only through `handOverJob` (`services/bots/jobs.ts`); requests from Users also pass `requireRoom` (at most 3 Jobs in progress per Organization).
- A Routine hands the same Job over every week or month; `startRoutineClock` checks once a minute in the server process (ADR 0010); on Vercel a daily Cron Job calls `/api/cron/routines` instead (ADR 0012).
- A Connection is an entry in `services/connections.ts` (kind, category, status) plus its copy on the Integrações page.
- Changing the GraphQL schema or operations: run `pnpm codegen`.
- Changing the Drizzle schema: run `pnpm db:generate`, then `pnpm db:migrate`.
- Changing scoring rules in `assessment.ts`: bump `ASSESSMENT_MODEL_VERSION`. The scale (0–1000, SPC bands, 4.966 Stages) is fixed by ADR 0005.

## Commands

- `pnpm tui` (`scripts/tui.mts`): `.env`, Docker, install, Postgres, migrations and codegen, then server and client in one screen (`--skip-install`). See `docs/local-development.md`.
- `pnpm infra:up`, then `pnpm db:migrate`, then `pnpm dev`: Postgres in Docker, apps on the host.
- `docker compose up --build`: everything in containers.
- Bots need `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`. Without it the Bot panels explain what is missing.
- Server tests (`apps/server/src/__tests__`) use a real Postgres database, `zapbots_test`, created and migrated on demand, so they need `pnpm infra:up`. External APIs are faked with MSW (`apps/server/test/msw`); our own code is not mocked. One test file per feature.
- `pnpm test:unit` · `pnpm lint:tsc` · `pnpm lint`
